A second story

A library is a collection of invitations. Some lead to places we already know, while others point towards an unfamiliar horizon. The best part of keeping a reading list is knowing that a new beginning is always close at hand.

This second story gives us a chance to follow a link, take a detour, and then find our way back. Reading should make that movement feel natural, with the same simple controls available whenever we need them.

When we return to an earlier page, it should be waiting just as we left it. The thread of our attention matters more than any particular screen. A good reader helps us hold on to that thread.
